Research Roadmap

A program of testable questions.

Our work begins with a reproducible implementation, then examines whether retained, evidence-backed experience improves decisions over time.

Current status

Foundations are complete. WIM-EB-001 establishes an operational engineering baseline for the exercised local profile, and Phase 3 research preparation is now authorized. Candidate certification remains closed, Red Team Review 03 remains incomplete with no determination, and EXP-0001 has not begun. Real-world performance benefits remain unproven.

Reviewed 22 September 2026

How WIM becomes a trustworthy research system

Publication accompanies the program whenever methods or evidence are mature enough to share. These phases describe research development, not prerequisites for publication.

  1. Phase 1

    Foundations

    Complete

    Define the mission, ontology, specification, and governance needed to state the research program precisely.

  2. Phase 2

    Engineering Baseline

    Operational

    Establish a reproducible reference implementation, deterministic replay, trust enforcement, and a private World capable of supporting WIM’s own development. This baseline is an engineering subject for research, not a certified Candidate or scientific result.

  3. Phase 3

    Scientific Readiness

    In Progress

    Build a defensible research environment before real data enters the system. EXP-0001 remains at the end of this path and requires a separate authorization after the exact research subject passes readiness review.

  4. Phase 4

    First Empirical Study

    Planned

    Run EXP-0001 against permitted, temporally bounded historical data and declared baselines. Preserve null, adverse, contradicted, and leakage-invalidated results alongside favorable evidence.

  5. Phase 5

    Longitudinal Learning

    Planned

    Measure whether governed experience improves defined work over time, and whether lessons remain accurate as outcomes accumulate.

  6. Phase 6

    Transfer and Replication

    Planned

    Test whether any measured effect survives across tasks, Worlds, time periods, and reasoning models. Seek independent reproduction and preserve discrepancies.

  7. Phase 7

    Simulation Research

    Planned

    Use bounded simulations to generate hypotheses and stress cases after a real-world validation path exists. Simulation remains separate from evidence about reality.

  8. Phase 8

    Publication and Research Community

    Planned

    Publish methods, evidence, failures, benchmarks, and reproducibility materials when each is ready. Support independent evaluation, replication, and governed external contribution.

Product development follows a separate roadmap. MeetWIM does not define WIM’s research status, evidence, or architecture.
