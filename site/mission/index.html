Mission

How should AI learn from experience?

WIM studies how AI systems can retain experience, revise their conclusions, and carry useful knowledge into future work.

Research purpose

AI systems often work from temporary context. Evidence can become separated from a conclusion, completed work can be mistaken for a successful outcome, and a lesson can outlive the conditions that once supported it.

The World Intelligence Model is a research program for maintaining an evidence-backed record of a changing world. It asks whether persistent, evidence-backed context can make future work more useful and more honest—and under which conditions the added complexity is justified.

The question is deliberately bounded. A maintained record is useful only when it preserves scope, time, provenance, and uncertainty. Continuity therefore means more than recall: a reader should be able to trace where a conclusion came from, what evidence was available at the time, and what later caused it to change.

Learning from experience

WIM connects what a system observed with what it concluded, decided, and did. It also records what happened afterward. This creates a traceable loop for learning:

  1. Observationsprovide evidence.
  2. Evidenceinforms a model of the world.
  3. The modelinforms decisions and actions.
  4. Outcomesprovide grounds to retain, narrow, revise, or reject conclusions.

The program is developing and evaluating this architecture. Its benefits have not yet been established through real-world experiments.

Evidence and revision

WIM maintains models of reality; it does not define reality. Evidence, interpretation, confidence, and truth remain distinct. Sources and time boundaries stay visible so that a later reader can understand what was known when a decision was made.

New evidence adds to the record. It can support an earlier conclusion, expose a contradiction, or show that a lesson no longer applies. Revision should preserve that history instead of silently replacing it.

Long-term commitment

The work is designed to survive changes in models, vendors, products, and institutions. Reproducibility, privacy, human authority, and clear limits remain part of the research question. Failures and negative results belong in the record alongside successful work.

WIM's long-term aim is to make accumulated machine knowledge traceable, revisable, and useful.
