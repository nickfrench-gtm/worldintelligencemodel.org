Governance

Clear authority. Accountable decisions.

WIM's governance defines who can change the system, what evidence supports a claim, and how corrections become part of the record.

Current assurance status

The governance framework has been adopted. The current implementation has not passed its assurance gate, so adoption must not be read as demonstrated compliance.

Reviewed 22 September 2026

Who defines WIM?

The Mission sets the program's purpose. The WIM Specification defines the system's concepts and behavior. Implementations are expected to follow it; they cannot redefine WIM.

Mission
Sets the purpose and long-term commitments of the research program.
Specification
Defines WIM's concepts and how they relate. Implementations are expected to follow it.
Trust Invariants
Set the trust requirements an implementation must satisfy, including separation of scope, evidence integrity, correct treatment of time, and preservation of history.
Implementation
Turns the Specification into software and produces reproducible evidence about its behavior.
Research
Tests whether WIM's ideas hold up in defined tasks and reports results, uncertainty, failures, and limits.
Publication
Shares research when its evidence, method, and limits are ready for public review.

How are changes reviewed?

Implementation evidence moves through distinct forms of review. Each stage asks a different question; passing one does not replace the next.

  1. ImplementationBuild within the approved specification.
  2. VerificationCheck required behavior and reproducibility.
  3. Red TeamAttempt to falsify trust-boundary claims.
  4. Independent auditAssess the exact candidate and its evidence.
  5. Candidate considerationReview whether every required gate has passed.
  6. ReleaseRequires every review gate to pass.

When review finds a failure: bounded remediation is followed by a fresh Red Team review before independent audit can resume. The earlier failure remains part of the record.

What does verification establish?

Tests and reproducible builds show whether software behaves as specified. Red Team review tries to break its trust claims. Independent audit then evaluates the exact candidate and its evidence before release.

Software correctness and scientific usefulness are different questions. Conformance evidence cannot establish that WIM improves reasoning, decisions, or learning.

How are research claims evaluated?

Research claims are judged by their methods and evidence, not by whether the code shipped. Results are reported at the level they have reached, with uncertainty and limits intact.

Failed, null, contradicted, and invalidated work stays in the record. Unknown is preferred over unsupported certainty.

How are errors recorded?

Corrections add to history rather than replacing it. Superseded decisions point to what replaced them, and failed reviews remain visible.

WIM maintains models of reality; it does not define reality. Evidence and outcomes may support, narrow, contradict, or leave a conclusion unresolved. Reality remains the final authority.
